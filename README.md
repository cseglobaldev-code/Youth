# Y.O.U — Youth Organizations United

Y.O.U is a global alliance connecting youth-led organizations across continents to advance sustainable development, global diplomacy, and meaningful change.

## Mission

Build bridges between countries, cultures, and generations — where every young person has the platform and tools to lead positive change in their community.

## Goals

- Connect 50+ member organizations across 6 continents and 30+ countries
- Support 1,500+ volunteers and young leaders worldwide
- Deliver projects aligned with the UN’s 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
- Create opportunities for collaboration, capacity building, and resource sharing among youth organizations

## Two ways to join

**For organizations** — Register as an official Y.O.U member, access a global partner network, join joint programs, and attend the Annual Summit.

**For individuals** — Apply for Continental Director or Country Director roles and take on formal leadership within the alliance’s global structure.
